## Hypotheses

These are strategic readings, written here in this pack's own words. They are not this company's
stated position, none of them is load-bearing, and none may be quoted as a fact about anybody.

- The durable advantage sits in the operating scaffold built around a model rather than in the choice of model, and three years of iteration are what is stored there. [HYPOTHESIS]
- The stated destination, an agent that carries the whole customer relationship, is now further ahead of the shipped surface than it is ahead of the articulation, so the distance left to close is depth of execution rather than clarity of intent. [HYPOTHESIS]
- The binding constraint is not product quality but how far a company of this size and funding shape can travel before better-capitalised entrants take the tier above it. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Four constraints compound on the way to the next order of magnitude: capital, holding the segment already won, brand reach beyond one founder, and organisational depth. [HYPOTHESIS]
- A self-serve-only motion had a lower ceiling than the plan assumed, and adding a sales track beside it was the lever that restarted growth. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Metering the bill on consumption is a forecasting risk for the buyer, because next month cannot be read off the pricing page, while the tier above prices per outcome instead. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Gross margin and net revenue retention are the two undisclosed numbers that decide whether this base compounds, since the cost of inference sits between what the customer pays and what the model provider charges. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Two forces test the scaffold advantage from opposite directions: model vendors moving up the stack, and open agent frameworks making a build-it-in-house answer cheaper each quarter. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Breadth of surface at this headcount risks doing several things adequately rather than one thing decisively, and the positioning ambiguity buyers report is the outward symptom of that tension. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Consolidation is running from the top of the category through acquisition, which sharpens the competitive threat and simultaneously puts a public price on a proven book of self-serve business. [HYPOTHESIS]
- A capital-light software company has historically plateaued somewhere in the low-to-mid nine figures of revenue, and clearing that band is the exception rather than the pattern. [HYPOTHESIS]
- The two-year outcome turns on one variable: whether enterprise credibility arrives before the larger players seal the tier above. [HYPOTHESIS]

## Evidence bar

- **FOUND.** A public figure restated here in this pack's own wording, carrying one of the three

  defect rather than a shortcut.
- **CALCULATED.** Arithmetic over figures drawn from one statement, with the chain written next to
  the result. Arithmetic across two sources is not calculation, it is construction.
- **HYPOTHESIS.** A strategic reading. Never load-bearing, never a fact about a named company, and
  never carried into a deliverable body.

Before anything on this page is used:

1. **It is a benchmark, never a subject.** These figures describe how one business of this shape was
   built. Set beside a plan they read as a target, and they are not one.
2. **All money figures are in US dollars,** and this is a subscription business with no marketplace
   volume and no transaction revenue underneath the revenue lines.
3. **The undisclosed set is the important set.** No resolution rate, no net revenue retention, no
   average contract value, and no split of revenue between self-serve and sales-led. Those four
   would settle most of the questions this page raises, and none of them is public. Anything
   resting on them is unconfirmed, not merely unstated.
4. **Retention here counts accounts, not revenue.** An account-retention figure says nothing about
   expansion or downgrade inside the accounts that stayed, and the two move independently.
5. **Three figure classes are deliberately untagged and therefore absent above,** because none of
   the three permitted descriptors covers them and a fourth descriptor invented for convenience
