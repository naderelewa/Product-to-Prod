## How to read a line

This file is a figure ledger, not an argument. Every number below is restated in this package's own
words — no sentence here is anybody else's sentence — and carries two things: the CLASS of evidence
behind it, and the year that number describes. The class is named instead of an address on purpose.
An address rots quietly; a class keeps telling a reader how much weight the figure can carry long
after any link behind it has died.

- **public company reporting** — the public record on a company: material it published about itself,
  and third-party reporting of it. Where a line names a comparison company, the class describes the
  public record on that company, not on the subject of this pack.
- **public filings** — a regulated disclosure by a listed company. The strongest class in this file,
  and the rarest, because the subject here is private.
- **founder interviews as publicly reported** — a founder speaking on the record, restated by
  somebody else, so it carries the speaker's incentive as well as the speaker's knowledge.

**Reported is not modelled.** Figures this company has never disclosed — margins, the paid-customer
count, the revenue mix — appear only as third-party estimates, tagged INFERRED with the estimating step
written beside them. They are not FOUND here at any strength, and no arithmetic in this file takes
one as an input.

The `§` pointer names the section of the benchmark corpus a line was read from. That corpus is the
single pointer in this pack's manifest, and it resolves only where an operator has materialised it;
until then it is a named input-readiness miss. The source class, never the pointer, is what grounds
the line.

Nothing here is a target. These are another company's numbers, at its stage, in its pricing model.
They are here for shape and for ordering, and never as a pass mark for anybody else.

## Hypotheses

## Evidence bar

Before a figure in this file shapes anybody's plan, a reader has to be able to see which of six
statuses it carries, and why that status and not a stronger one.

- **FOUND.** Reported publicly, with the class of source and the year on the same line. Every FOUND
  line here names one of the three classes above. A number that cannot be classed is not FOUND in
  this file at any strength, and is left out rather than quietly promoted.
- **INFERRED.** A third-party estimate of something this company never disclosed. The line still names
  the class the estimate was carried in and the year it describes, alongside the estimating step,
  because an estimate that hides its own provenance cannot be told apart from a guess. Margins, the
  early revenue path and the competitor comparison are the whole of this class here, and they are
  the weakest evidence in the file.
- **CONSTRUCTED.** Built for illustration rather than measured. This file holds none. A benchmark
  that invents a figure to close a gap has stopped being a benchmark.
- **CALCULATED.** Arithmetic on stated inputs drawn from one source class, with the inputs and the
  date offset shown. Two lines qualify, and each names its offset instead of smoothing it away.
- **HYPOTHESIS.** The strategic readings above. They are restated arguments, not findings, and a run
  that cites one as a fact about this company or any other has misread the file.
- **NEEDS-CONFIRMATION.** Load-bearing and unanswerable from public material. Each one names its
  confirmer and what it blocks, so it reads as work rather than as a caveat.

This ledger is trusted only while all six of the following hold.

1. Every figure carries its own source class and its own year, and no line borrows the class of the line above it.
2. Reported and modelled never merge: a figure the company has never disclosed stays INFERRED however often it is repeated elsewhere.
3. No arithmetic crosses source classes, and none crosses a date boundary without naming the offset in the same line.
4. Nothing is read as a target. A single company's number set beside your own reads as a goal, and this one is not.
5. The dated lines are re-read before they are reused. Everything here describes a two-year stretch of hypergrowth, which is the shortest-lived kind of fact there is.
6. Known blanks stay written down as blanks, not filled with the nearest adjacent number.
