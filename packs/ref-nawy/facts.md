## Hypotheses

The lines below are this pack's own strategic reading of the company, written from public
information and owned by this pack alone. None of them is measured, none is stated by the company,
and none may bear load in a deliverable. They are questions to put to a run's own evidence, not
findings about this business.

- [HYPOTHESIS] The gap that matters is between an ambition stated as a data and technology business and a public product whose surface is still that of a person-led selling operation.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The product is arranged around the catalogue rather than around the person: nothing routes a user by intent, so a first-time off-plan buyer, a yield seeker, a resale-minded speculator, an overseas hard-asset saver, an existing owner, and a broker all arrive in the same state, each needing an entry point of its own, its own proof that the platform can be trusted, and its own test of what a good outcome would look like.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The separation between the lines is partly a regulatory fact, since the lender, the investment vehicle, and the brokerage are separately licensed and separately capitalised; the fair critique is the missing connective tissue across them, a single identity, a single history, and recommendations that read across every line, rather than the perimeters themselves.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The largest move available is the shift from one-time transaction commission to recurring engagement across an ownership lifecycle, and it requires no new product, only the existing ones behaving as one.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The fractional-investment surface answers what a unit is but not what it returns; a net yield, a projected return, and a comparison against a deposit or against gold are the missing inputs at the point of decision, and the company already holds the data that would supply them.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The data advantage is real and has no external expression: proprietary demand, supply, and transaction data that no regional competitor can assemble at this scale surfaces to users as neither valuation, nor affordability guidance, nor yield estimate.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Part of that gap is structural rather than a matter of product will, because a Gulf rival can publish prices and valuations where a public transaction registry exists, while in this market the ground truth has to be assembled privately before it can be surfaced at all.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The ceiling is not saturation: the overwhelming majority of transactions in this market still complete elsewhere, so the constraint is coverage and routing rather than demand.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The buyer experience clears the local market baseline convincingly and still sits below the level at which the experience itself, rather than the inventory behind it, would differentiate.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The precedent path abroad ran listings first, then consumer-facing valuations, then a licensed data product sold to banks and government, with lending arriving as a relationship wedge rather than as a near-term revenue line, and expansion earned only once the core was the undisputed default.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The open question underneath all of it is which of four coherent futures is being chosen, a regional transaction platform, a cross-border wealth platform, an ownership companion, or a market-intelligence layer, since each of the four carries a cost base of its own, a hiring profile of its own, and a defensibility of its own.

## Evidence bar

A benchmark earns trust only when a reader can see where each number came from and what it is not
allowed to do. This file is trusted while all of the following hold.

1. Every figure names a class of public source and a year. The class is a kind of source, never a
   document; a figure that cannot name its class does not enter the file.
2. FOUND here means read from public information in that year, not audited. The scale figures are
   company statements repeated in public reporting, and GMV is not revenue: no margin, profit, or
   take-rate figure sits behind any number above.
3. Ranges stay ranges. Both market-share lines above were published as bands rather than as single
   values, and a midpoint written as a point estimate is a new claim rather than a tidier one.
4. Two denominators live in this file and never cross. Share by transaction count and share by GMV
   value are different numbers about the same company in the same year, and either one quoted as
   market share without saying which denominator it used is unusable.
5. Hypotheses bear no load. They are the questions to put to a run's own data, they never migrate
   into a deliverable as facts, and they are stripped at delivery.
6. Known blanks are written down as blanks: no disclosed revenue, margin, or EBITDA; no cohort-typed
   retention or repeat-purchase rate; no unit economics or take-rate per product line; no disclosed
   acquisition cost; and no public split of volume between primary sale, resale, and rental. Those
   are open questions against your own data, not gaps to fill with the nearest adjacent number.
7. Read for shape, for ordering, and for population ranges within this one company. A benchmark is
   somebody else's answer to somebody else's question, and it is never another company's target.

This file is the pack's standing record. The pack's corpus pointer may resolve to nothing on a given
machine; that is a named miss at input-readiness, never an error, and never a reason to soften a
line written here.
