## Hypotheses

The lines above are the record. Everything below is a reading of them, written in this pack's own
words, owned by this pack and by nobody else, and none of it is the company's claim.

- [HYPOTHESIS] The advantage is not that the company out-built the incumbent brokers but that it manufactured a retail investing market where one barely existed, which means its share statistics measure a category it created rather than one it took.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The distance between a publicly stated four-service ambition, covering investing, borrowing, paying and insuring, and a product shipping one of those services with a second in approval, is the central strategic exposure rather than a communications problem.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Switching costs have been designed and announced but not shipped, so the interval between announcement and live-at-scale is exactly the interval in which a better-distributed rival can act.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The product is strongest where a user has invested nothing yet and weakest where a user has money at stake, which inverts the usual relationship between a user's effort and the value returned to them.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The gap between the two store ratings is a platform-execution signal rather than a difference in taste, and it sits on the operating system the mass-market user actually carries, which makes the lower-rated platform the more representative reading.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Payments firms adding investing and lending firms adding funds are assembling the same destination from the opposite end, and the daily-spending habit is the harder half to acquire, so the side starting from payments may hold the stickier ground.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The distribution threat from a state bank needs no better app: an adequate investing tab attached to an existing deposit relationship can take the saver segment on trust and near-zero acquisition cost.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Single-product rivals can each carve away one slice, a sharper cash yield here, an already-profitable automated portfolio there, a longer-running property platform beside them, and hollow out the platform claim without ever beating it head-on, leaving behind a competent broker with a few extras attached.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Heavy investment in first-time investor education creates a graduate population that a later, better-resourced entrant can compete for without paying the education cost, so retention is the only defence against having subsidised somebody else's funnel.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Cutting fund commissions while deepening subscriptions is a bet that recurring subscription revenue plus fees on a growing asset base outgrow per-trade income, and the bet is exposed if asset growth slows while subscription churn rises in the same window.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Founder fluency with one home market does not transfer automatically to the neighbouring ones, so expansion quality is an open question rather than a scheduling question.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Three things have to arrive together for the platform claim to become true: a memory layer that acts on a portfolio rather than suggesting to it, one identity across markets, and a credit product. Any two of them leave the business on transaction fees.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Being publicly understood as the exchange app is itself a product problem, because a newcomer cannot tell from the surface whether it replaces a broker, a savings account or an adviser.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The licences, the fund assets and the user base are distribution rails other institutions could rent, and moving first on that rail is a defence against the same rails being built around the company.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Over a two-year horizon the deciding variable is a race rather than a capability: whether durable retention arrives before a rival assembles an adequate full wallet.

## The evidence bar

Before any figure above is repeated in a document that points spend, pricing or engineering, it clears these gates.

1. **Every figure carries a class and a year.** A number with no class beside it is not a benchmark, it is a rumour with a decimal point, and a number with no year cannot be seen to have gone stale.
2. **This is a secondary read, not a primary verification.** Every figure was taken from a public statement about the company rather than from the company's records, and none of it was re-opened at its original publisher. A figure that becomes load-bearing is re-read at its source first, or it drops a tag level and says so in the same line.
3. **What the company says about itself is marked as such.** Assets, investor counts, order share, support quality and product surface are the company's own account; the share of traded value, the brokerage ranking, the exchange growth and the licensing states sit in filings. Naming a figure as the company's own describes its provenance and does not doubt it.
4. **Population travels in the prose, class travels in the citation.** A share of one exchange's order flow is not a share of a region, a rating count from one storefront is not a rating count from a market, and an employer platform with a few dozen reviews is a direction rather than a measurement.
5. **Companies are named; individuals are not.** The companies are named openly, because a benchmark whose subject is hidden is a number with no referent. People are carried as a source class instead, so what the founders have said in public becomes a class rather than a name.
6. **Editorial scoring is not carried here.** A score assigned to this product on somebody's own scale is a judgment rather than a company or public statement, and a judgment with no class to stand on does not enter a fact register.
7. **A figure whose class could not be named was dropped.** Two figures in the underlying material were left out on that ground rather than filed under the nearest class that would take them, because the nearest class is how a benchmark quietly becomes fiction.
8. **The reading is quarantined from the record.** Every claim about what these figures mean sits under the hypotheses heading, in this pack's words, and stays a hypothesis until somebody puts it against a decision they actually own.
9. **Nothing here grounds a claim about your own product.** A benchmark is off-population until proven on-population: this is one company, in one market, with a consumer base built up from almost no retail investing at all. Borrowing its conversion or retention shape for a different population is the error this file exists to make visible.
10. **The record behind these figures is not shipped with them, and they have a review date.** The pack's corpus pointer is expected to resolve to nothing, which is a named miss and never an error; and figures describing a company mid-way through a licensing queue need re-reading before they are quoted a second year.
